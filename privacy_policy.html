Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GoldGen Auto Poster ("we", "our", or "the application") collects, uses, and handles information when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate the service:

  • Facebook Page Access Token — provided by you to allow posting on your behalf.
  • Facebook Page ID — used to identify the page where content is published.
  • Post data — records of published posts stored locally for tracking purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To automatically publish gold price content to your Facebook Page.
  • To read posts and comments on your page for auto-reply functionality.
  • To track posting history and display statistics in the dashboard.

3. Facebook Permissions Used

This application requests the following Facebook permissions:

  • pages_show_list — to access your page information.
  • pages_manage_posts — to publish posts to your page.
  • pages_read_engagement — to read engagement data (likes, shares).
  • pages_read_user_content — to read comments on your posts.
  • pages_manage_engagement — to reply to comments on your posts.

4. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on our server. We do not share, sell, or transfer your data to any third parties. Access tokens are stored securely and used solely for the purposes described above.

5. Data Retention

Post history and logs are retained for operational purposes. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

6. Third-Party Services

This application interacts with the following third-party services:

  • Meta (Facebook) Graph API — for publishing and reading page content.
  • Google Gemini AI — for generating post content and images.

Use of these services is subject to their respective privacy policies.

7. Your Rights

You may revoke the application's access to your Facebook Page at any time through your Facebook Settings under "Apps and Websites". Upon revocation, we will no longer be able to post or read content on your behalf.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the application dashboard at gold.kelasmaster.id.
